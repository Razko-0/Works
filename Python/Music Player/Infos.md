Since I don't have spotify premium, I download my musics from [Soundcloud](https://soundcloud.com) via [MusicVerter](https://www.musicverter.com/).

I am doing an 'application' version with pygame.

You can always replace the 'folder' variable with the path where you have all your musics, so you don't need an additional 'Music' folder.
